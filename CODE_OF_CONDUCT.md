# Code of conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone.

Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable feedback, and keep technical disagreement focused on the work. Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate exposure of private information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's security-advisory channel. Maintainers will review reports promptly, protect reporter privacy where possible, and act proportionately.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when representing the project in public community spaces.
